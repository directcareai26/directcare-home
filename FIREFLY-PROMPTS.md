## Reusable styling appendix

If Firefly results feel too "stocky" or generic, add these tail clauses to any prompt:

- `..., shot on Sony A7IV, 85mm prime, f/1.8, hyperreal skin texture, magazine cover quality, premium editorial brand photography`
- `..., natural skin texture, no airbrushing, candid expression, premium wellness brand aesthetic`
- `..., negative space top right for headline placement, soft vignette, premium D2C healthcare brand style`

If a category's photo needs to feel cooler/more clinical:
- Add: `clinical but warm aesthetic, hint of brand lavender purple in props, premium telehealth brand`

If a category needs to feel warmer/more lifestyle:
- Add: `relaxed candid lifestyle moment, sunlight, no medical clichés, premium wellness brand photography`

## After generating

1. Save each image at **2x resolution** (Firefly's "Variations" → "2x Upscale").
2. Run through **TinyPNG** or **Squoosh.app** at 80% quality → most images should land 80-180KB.
3. Replace the Unsplash URLs in `index.html`:
   - The hero clinician → `<img src="..." class="hero-visual img" />`
   - The 9 offering grid cards → `.offer-img-wrap img`
   - The 3 testimonial avatars → `.testi .who img`
   - The 2 gender split cards → `.split-card img`
4. Once images are ready, drop them in the same folder as `index.html` and reference like `/hero-clinician.jpg`. Tell me when ready and I'll wire them in and redeploy.
