## ⚠️ What copy can and cannot do (read before shipping)

Copy can remove steering language, add disclosure duties, and state clinician independence.
**Copy cannot make a payment structure lawful.** The fact pattern counsel scrutinises is:

> a **recurring**, **per-patient** commission, paid to a **non-clinician**, that **varies by
> which service the patient ends up receiving** ($75 GLP-1 / $45 TRT / $30 ED / $30 HRT / $10 hair).

Payment that tracks *what was prescribed* is the part that draws scrutiny, and no wording
changes it. The copy below is the most defensible presentation of the program **as it is
currently structured**. If counsel wants the structure de-risked rather than just described,
the usual remedies are: a **flat fee per qualified lead** regardless of service, or a **flat
monthly marketing fee at fair market value** — neither of which varies by prescription.
That is a program-terms change in FlexOffers, not a copy change. Raise it with counsel.

## PARTNER RULES — required on the page, not buried in a PDF

**You must disclose that you are paid.** Every time you promote DirectCare AI — in the same
post, video, message or conversation, before the link — say clearly that you earn a commission
on referrals. "#ad", "paid partner", or "I get paid if you sign up" all work. This is required
by the FTC and it is a condition of the program.

**You are not a clinician and must not act like one.** You may not give medical advice,
interpret anyone's labs or symptoms, recommend a specific medication or dose, tell anyone they
qualify for a program, or suggest anyone stop or change a treatment.

**Never promise a result.** Do not claim or imply any outcome — weight lost, hormone levels,
hair regrown, or performance of any kind.

**Never position DirectCare AI as a performance aid.** DirectCare AI programs are prescribed
only for clinical indications. You may not present any program as a physique, competition or
athletic-performance product, or imply it is an alternative to anything used for those purposes.

**Do not target individuals by health condition.** Promote to your audience generally. Do not
single out a specific person because you believe they have a medical condition.

**Do not speak for DirectCare AI.** You are an independent partner, not an employee, agent or
representative, and you cannot make commitments on our behalf.

Partners who do not follow these rules are removed from the program and forfeit unpaid
commissions.

> **Fixes M4.** This is the written policy counsel will ask whether you had, and the FTC
> Endorsement Guides expect an advertiser to instruct *and monitor* affiliates. It also
> converts the hardest line in B6 — coaches steering advisees into hormone therapy — into an
> explicit prohibition.

## CLINICIAN INDEPENDENCE — new section, add it

Referral commissions have no bearing on clinical decisions. Clinicians evaluate every patient
independently and prescribe only what is clinically appropriate for that person. Partners are
paid for marketing, not for any treatment decision, and a partner's commission never changes
what a clinician recommends. Not everyone who starts an evaluation qualifies for treatment.

> **Why:** the single worst reading of the payout table is that a third party has a continuing
> financial interest in a patient *staying on* therapy. This is the paragraph that says the
> quiet part is not true. It is also the paragraph counsel will most want to see.
