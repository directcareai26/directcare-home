About DirectCare AI

Modern healthcare, built around you.

DirectCare AI is a direct-to-patient telehealth platform delivering personalized, clinician-prescribed care — weight loss, hormones, sexual health, hair regrowth, supplements and labs — all in one place, with no insurance required.

Our Why

Healthcare shouldn't feel like a maze.

For too long, getting the care you actually need has meant fighting your insurance, waiting weeks for a 10-minute appointment, getting a referral, waiting again, paying a copay, then paying full retail at the pharmacy — and that's just to ask one question.

We built DirectCare AI because the modern patient deserves better. Care should be personal, transparent, and immediate. Prices should be visible up front. Your clinician should know your full history, not just the slice in front of them. And the medication you need should arrive at your door — not require three more phone calls.

One platform. One care team. One subscription. Your whole health, finally in one place.

What We Believe

Four things we won't compromise on.

These are the principles that shape every protocol, every prescription, and every conversation we have with a patient.

Personalized

Care is built around your labs, your history, and your goals — not a template. Protocols are titrated by your clinician, not picked from a drop-down.

Transparent

You see the full price before you ever click subscribe. No hidden fees, no surprise upcharges, no "call to find out." What you see is what you pay.

Accessible

No insurance. No referrals. No drive across town. Get evaluated, get prescribed, and get your medication shipped — usually within days, not weeks.

Continuous

Real care doesn't stop at the first refill. Your clinician follows up, adjusts your protocol, and is one message away — for as long as you're with us.

One Place For Everything

The care that used to take six providers, in one app.

Most of what keeps you feeling like yourself — your weight, your hormones, your energy, your skin, your sleep, your sex life — doesn't sit inside one specialty. So we don't make you book six different appointments to fix it.

DirectCare AI brings primary, preventive, and longevity-oriented care under one roof. One patient profile. One care team that talks to itself. One place to track everything.
