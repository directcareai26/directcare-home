Patient Rights

Patient Bill of Rights

Last Updated: April 7, 2026

This Patient Bill of Rights describes the rights and responsibilities of patients receiving care facilitated through the DirectCare AI Platform. It supplements (does not replace) any provider-specific consent forms, state-mandated patient rights notices, our Privacy Policy, and our Telehealth Informed Consent.

Independent providers, independent judgment. Care is delivered by independent licensed clinicians and clinical partners (including OpenLoop and the Meta Digital Medical Network). DirectCare AI does not practice medicine and does not control clinical decision-making.

I.Respect and Dignity

You have the right to be treated with respect, dignity, and consideration of your personal values, beliefs, and cultural background by every clinician and team member you interact with through DirectCare AI.

II.Information About Your Care

You have the right to:

  • Know the name and credentials of the clinician treating you.
  • Receive a clear explanation of your diagnosis, treatment options, expected outcomes, and reasonable alternatives — including the option of no treatment.
  • Ask questions and have them answered in language you can understand.
  • Receive information about any prescribed medication, including its purpose, dosage, common side effects, and risks.

III.Informed Consent

You have the right to give or withhold informed consent before any clinical service, prescription, or procedure. You have the right to know the benefits, risks, and alternatives of any treatment before agreeing to it. See our Telehealth Informed Consent for details specific to telehealth services.

IV.Right to Refuse Treatment

You have the right to refuse any treatment, medication, or service to the extent permitted by law. Your clinician will inform you of the medical consequences of any refusal.

V.Privacy and Confidentiality

You have the right to:

  • Privacy during clinical communications.
  • Confidentiality of your medical records and personal information, consistent with applicable federal and state law (including HIPAA).
  • Receive our Notice of Privacy Practices, which describes how protected health information is used and disclosed.

See also: Privacy Policy and (for California residents) CCPA Notice.

VI.Access to Your Health Information

You have the right to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of your medical records on file with DirectCare AI's affiliated clinical entities.
  • Request corrections to information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Receive an accounting of certain disclosures of your protected health information.

To request access, message your clinician through the patient portal or email privacy@directcareai.com.

VII.Continuity of Care

You have the right to:

  • Be informed of clinically reasonable continuing healthcare needs after a telehealth visit, refill, or program completion.
  • Receive timely follow-up communication when clinically appropriate.
  • Request transfer of your records to another clinician of your choice, consistent with applicable law.

VIII.Communication and Language

You have the right to receive information in a way you can understand. If you have limited English proficiency or a disability that affects communication, please notify us so reasonable accommodations can be arranged.

IX.Cost and Payment Transparency

You have the right to:

  • Receive clear information about the cost of services, medications, devices, and any subscription fees before purchase.
  • Receive an itemized statement upon request.
  • Be informed if a particular service is not covered by insurance, and that DirectCare AI is a direct-pay platform that does not bill insurance.

X.Freedom from Discrimination

You have the right to receive care without discrimination based on race, color, national origin, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, disability, source of payment, or any other basis protected by law.

XI.Right to Complain

You have the right to voice concerns or file complaints about the care you receive without fear of retaliation. To raise a concern:

  • Message your clinician through the patient portal.
  • Email contact@directcareai.com.
  • Call 888-298-6718.

To file a complaint against an individual clinician with a state medical board, see the state-specific links in our Telehealth Informed Consent.

XII.Patient Responsibilities

Along with the rights above, you also share responsibility for your care. You agree to:

  • Provide complete, accurate, and current information about your medical history, medications, allergies, and symptoms.
  • Follow the treatment plan your clinician has prescribed, or notify your clinician if you cannot or do not intend to follow it.
  • Keep scheduled appointments and lab draws, or cancel them with reasonable notice.
  • Pay for services in accordance with the financial agreement you accepted at sign-up.
  • Treat clinicians and staff with respect.
  • Seek emergency care immediately when symptoms require it.

XIII.Emergencies

DirectCare AI is not an emergency service. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. If you are in mental health crisis, dial or text 988 or visit 988Lifeline.org.
