## Food Order: Vegetables and Protein Before Carbs

 In a small 2015 study of adults with type 2 diabetes, eating vegetables and protein before the carbohydrate in the same meal lowered blood sugar by 37% at one hour.

 In a 2015 Weill Cornell pilot study of 11 adults with type 2 diabetes, eating the vegetables and protein of a meal 15 minutes before the carbohydrate lowered blood sugar by 29% at 30 minutes, 37% at 60 minutes and 17% at 120 minutes, compared with eating the carbohydrate first. The meal was identical both times.

 Most advice about blood sugar is about what to eat and how much. A research group at Weill Cornell Medical College asked a different question: with the same food on the plate, does the order you eat it in change anything?

#### What the study did

 Eleven adults with type 2 diabetes, all with overweight or obesity and all taking a common oral diabetes medication, ate the same meal on two mornings a week apart. The meal was grilled chicken breast, a lettuce and tomato salad with vinaigrette, steamed broccoli with butter, ciabatta bread and orange juice.

- Visit one: bread and juice first, then the chicken and vegetables 15 minutes later

- Visit two: chicken and vegetables first, then the bread and juice 15 minutes later

 Blood sugar and insulin were measured before the meal and at 30, 60 and 120 minutes.

#### What it found

 When the carbohydrate came last, blood sugar was lower at every time point:

- 29% lower at 30 minutes

- 37% lower at 60 minutes

- 17% lower at 120 minutes

 Insulin levels at one and two hours were also significantly lower, so the body needed less insulin to handle the same meal.

 Keep it in proportion

 This was a pilot study of 11 people, measured for two hours after one meal. The participants had type 2 diabetes, and results may be smaller in people without it. It did not measure long-term blood sugar control.

#### Why order could matter

 Fiber, protein and fat slow the rate at which the stomach empties. When they arrive first, the carbohydrate that follows is absorbed more gradually, and the rise in blood sugar is flatter.

#### How to try it

- Start with the salad or cooked vegetables and the protein

- Leave bread, rice, pasta or potatoes for the second half of the meal

- At restaurants, ask for the bread basket to come with the main course

- Have fruit or dessert at the end of a meal, not alone on an empty stomach

#### When to talk with a clinician

 If you take insulin or medication that can cause low blood sugar, talk with your clinician before changing how you eat.
