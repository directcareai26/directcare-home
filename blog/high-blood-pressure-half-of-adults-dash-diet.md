## High Blood Pressure: Nearly Half of US Adults, and What the DASH Diet Does

 The CDC estimates 48.1% of US adults have high blood pressure and about 1 in 4 of them has it controlled. In the DASH trial, diet alone lowered systolic pressure 11 points in adults with hypertension.

 The CDC estimates that 48.1% of US adults, about 119.9 million people, have high blood pressure, and about 1 in 4 of them has it under control. In the DASH trial, a diet rich in fruits, vegetables and low-fat dairy lowered blood pressure by 5.5/3.0 mm Hg overall and by 11.4/5.5 mm Hg in adults with hypertension, compared with a control diet.

 High blood pressure rarely causes symptoms, which is why it is so often missed and so often left uncontrolled.

#### How common it is

 The CDC estimates that 48.1% of US adults, about 119.9 million people, have high blood pressure. That figure uses the current definition: a top number above 130 mm Hg, a bottom number above 80 mm Hg, or taking medication for blood pressure.

 Only about 1 in 4 adults with high blood pressure, 22.5%, has it under control.

#### What the DASH trial tested

 DASH stands for Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension. In the original trial, 459 adults were fed one of three diets:

- A control diet typical of American eating

- The same diet with more fruits and vegetables

- A combination diet rich in fruits, vegetables and low-fat dairy, with less saturated and total fat

#### What it found

 Compared with the control diet, the combination diet lowered:

- Systolic pressure by 5.5 mm Hg and diastolic by 3.0 mm Hg across all participants

- Systolic pressure by 11.4 mm Hg and diastolic by 5.5 mm Hg among the 133 participants with hypertension

 The fruits-and-vegetables diet alone lowered systolic pressure by 2.8 mm Hg.

 Keep it in proportion

 This was a feeding trial with meals provided, so results at home depend on how closely the pattern is followed. Diet works alongside prescribed treatment and does not replace it.

#### What the pattern looks like

- Vegetables or fruit at every meal

- Low-fat dairy daily

- Whole grains, beans, nuts and seeds often

- Fish and poultry more often than red and processed meat

- Less salt, sweets and sugary drinks
