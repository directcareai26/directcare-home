## Sauna Use and Heart Health: The 20-Year Finnish Study

 Finnish men who used a sauna 4 to 7 times a week had a 63% lower risk of sudden cardiac death over 20 years than men who went once a week.

 In a study of 2,315 middle-aged Finnish men followed for a median of 20.7 years, those who used a sauna 4 to 7 times a week had a 63% lower risk of sudden cardiac death than those who used it once a week, after adjustment for cardiovascular risk factors. Similar associations were seen for fatal heart disease and all-cause mortality.

 In Finland, sauna bathing is a routine part of life, which gives researchers a population in which almost everyone does it and the main difference is how often.

#### What the study did

 The study followed 2,315 middle-aged men in eastern Finland for a median of 20.7 years. At the start, 601 reported one sauna session a week, 1,513 reported two to three, and 201 reported four to seven.

#### What it found

 Over the follow-up period, the share of men who died from any cause was:

- 49.1% among those with one session a week

- 37.8% among those with two to three sessions

- 30.8% among those with four to seven sessions

 After adjusting for cardiovascular risk factors, the risk of sudden cardiac death was 22% lower with two to three sessions a week and 63% lower with four to seven, compared with one. Similar patterns were found for fatal coronary heart disease, fatal cardiovascular disease and all-cause mortality.

 Keep it in proportion

 The study included only men, in a country where saunas are traditional dry saunas used from childhood. It is observational: men who use a sauna often may be healthier, less stressed or better off. It does not show that starting sauna use lowers risk.

#### Why heat might help

 During a sauna session, heart rate rises and blood vessels widen, in ways that resemble light to moderate exercise. Researchers have proposed effects on blood pressure and blood vessel function. A sauna does not replace exercise.

#### If you use a sauna

- Drink water before and after

- Avoid alcohol before and during

- Keep sessions moderate and leave if you feel dizzy or unwell

- Cool down gradually

#### Who should ask first

 People with unstable heart disease, recent heart attack, low blood pressure or fainting episodes, and people who are pregnant, should ask a licensed clinician before using a sauna.
