## Sildenafil vs. tadalafil: what each PDE5 inhibitor actually does differently

 Same drug class, very different drugs. Onset, duration, food interaction, and side-effect profile all matter. And the right one for you depends less on marketing and more on your lifestyle.

 Compares sildenafil (Viagra) vs. tadalafil (Cialis): sildenafil kicks in 30-60 min and lasts 4-6 hours, tadalafil takes 30-60 min and lasts 24-36 hours — daily dosing changes the math.

 Sildenafil and tadalafil are the two most-prescribed PDE5 inhibitors in the world. They're often described as interchangeable. They're not.

 Both relax smooth muscle in the corpus cavernosum by blocking phosphodiesterase-5, which allows for stronger and longer erections in response to sexual stimulation. But the pharmacokinetics. Onset, peak, half-life. Are different enough that picking the right one is genuinely a lifestyle question, not a clinical coin flip.

 Here's the side-by-side.

### Sildenafil (the Viagra molecule).

- Onset: 30 to 60 minutes when taken on an empty stomach.

- Peak effect: about 1 hour after dosing.

- Duration: 4 to 6 hours.

- Food interaction: A heavy meal. Especially anything fatty. Can delay absorption by 60+ minutes and blunt the peak. Take on an empty stomach if you can.

- Common dosing: 25 mg, 50 mg, or 100 mg as needed, no more than once per 24 hours.

 The use case sildenafil fits well: planned encounters where you can time the dose, you eat lighter that evening, and you don't need the medication active for the next 24 hours.

### Tadalafil (the Cialis molecule).

- Onset: 30 minutes to 2 hours.

- Peak effect: about 2 hours after dosing.

- Duration: up to 36 hours. (This is the headline difference.)

- Food interaction: Essentially none. You can eat normally.

- Common dosing: 5 mg, 10 mg, or 20 mg as needed; or 2.5 mg / 5 mg taken daily.

 The use case tadalafil fits well: you want spontaneity, you don't want to plan around a 30-minute window, or you'd rather take a low daily dose and have the medication always active.

 The headline difference

 Sildenafil is a moment medication. Tadalafil is a window medication. Pick based on which life you actually live.

### Side-effect profile.

 Both share the PDE5 side-effect family: headache, flushing, nasal congestion, mild GI upset, occasional visual changes. The key differences:

- Tadalafil more commonly causes back/muscle aches (related to its longer half-life and PDE-11 cross-reactivity). Usually resolves with continued use.

- Sildenafil more commonly causes the "blue tint" visual side effect, again because of PDE-6 cross-reactivity in the retina. Temporary and not dangerous, but real.

 Both are contraindicated with nitrate medications (used for chest pain). That combination can cause dangerous blood-pressure drops. Tell your clinician about every medication and supplement you take.

### Daily low-dose tadalafil: the underrated option.

 2.5 mg or 5 mg of tadalafil taken every day is a different experience than as-needed dosing. You stop thinking about ED medication. Side effects are typically milder because peak concentrations are lower. The trade-off is cost (more pills) and the commitment to daily dosing.

 For many men this turns out to be the cleanest answer. It also offers a small but real benefit for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) symptoms, which is why daily tadalafil is FDA-approved for that indication separately.
