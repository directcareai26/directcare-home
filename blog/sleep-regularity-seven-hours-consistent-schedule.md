## Sleep Regularity and the 7-Hour Mark: What the Research Shows

 A regular sleep schedule predicted mortality risk better than sleep duration in a 2024 study, about 7 hours was linked with the best thinking and mood, and at least 30% of adults in every US state fall short.

 In a 2024 study of 60,977 adults, higher sleep regularity was associated with a 20% to 48% lower risk of death from any cause, and regularity predicted risk better than sleep duration. A separate UK Biobank analysis found about 7 hours was the optimal sleep duration for cognition and mental health in adults aged 38 to 73.

 Sleep advice usually comes down to a number of hours. Recent research suggests two things matter: how long you sleep, and how consistent your timing is.

#### Regular timing

 Researchers calculated a Sleep Regularity Index for 60,977 UK adults, average age about 63, from wrist-worn activity trackers. The index measures how consistent sleep and wake times are from one day to the next. Participants were followed for an average of 6.3 years.

- Higher sleep regularity was associated with a 20% to 48% lower risk of death from any cause

- Sleep regularity was a stronger predictor of mortality risk than sleep duration

#### About seven hours

 A separate analysis of UK Biobank participants aged 38 to 73 looked at sleep duration alongside thinking tests, mental health measures and brain imaging. The relationship was curved. About 7 hours was the optimal duration, and both shorter and longer sleep were associated with poorer cognition and mental health, and with greater cognitive decline at follow-up.

#### How many adults fall short

 The CDC recommends at least 7 hours a night for adults. In its 2022 state data, the share of adults sleeping less than that ranged from 30% in Vermont to 46% in Hawaii, and the national picture did not improve between 2013 and 2022.

 Keep it in proportion

 Both studies are observational. Illness can disrupt sleep timing and lengthen sleep, so some of the link may run from health to sleep. Seven hours is an average finding, not a personal prescription.

#### How to make sleep more regular

- Pick a wake-up time and keep it within about an hour every day, weekends included

- Get outdoor light soon after waking

- Keep caffeine to the morning. Our article on [coffee timing](https://www.directcare.ai/blog/coffee-timing-morning-coffee-caffeine-cutoff) explains the 6-hour cutoff

- Keep the bedroom dark. See [sleeping with a light on](https://www.directcare.ai/blog/sleeping-with-light-on-heart-rate-insulin-resistance)

#### When to talk with a clinician

 Loud snoring with pauses in breathing, trouble sleeping at least three nights a week for three months, or sleeping nine or more hours and still feeling exhausted are worth an assessment.
