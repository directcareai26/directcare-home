Common questions

What you'd ask before ordering labs.

Where does the actual blood draw happen?
Two options. (1) Take your kit to a Quest Diagnostics or LabCorp location — both have walk-in slots and locations in most US zip codes; we email you the nearest. (2) Add a mobile phlebotomist for $25 — they come to your home and the draw takes about 15 minutes.
How long until I get results?
5–7 days after the draw. You'll get an email notification when results are ready. A clinician then reviews them with you via secure message in the patient portal — typically within 1–2 business days.
Do I need to fast?
For metabolic and cardiovascular panels — yes, 10–12 hours of fasting (water only) gives the cleanest read on glucose, insulin and triglycerides. Hormone panels don't require fasting, but draw early in the day (before 10 a.m.) for the most accurate testosterone reading.
Is this covered by insurance?
DirectCare AI labs are direct-pay — no insurance billing. That said, the cost is typically lower than the patient share for the same markers ordered through insurance, especially for advanced markers (ApoB, Lp(a), free T, etc.) that insurance often won't cover. HSA/FSA accepted.
What if a result is abnormal?
Your reviewing clinician will explain what it means, what's likely causing it, and what to do — including writing a prescription if appropriate. For urgent abnormalities (e.g., severely high blood sugar, dangerously low hemoglobin), we'll contact you directly.
Can I order labs without a DCAI protocol?
Yes. You don't need to be on TRT, HRT or any other DirectCare AI protocol to order labs. Many patients order labs annually as part of a personal preventive medicine routine.
