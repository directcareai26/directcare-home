What we test, by what you care about.

Every panel covers a full CBC, kidney and liver function. Here is what each goal looks at — and which panels include it.

Energy, weight & blood sugar

HbA1c, fasting insulin, glucose (+ uric acid in Essential)

  • HbA1c — Your average blood sugar over about 3 months.
  • Fasting insulin — How hard your body works to keep glucose steady.
  • Glucose — A single-moment blood sugar snapshot.
  • Uric acid — Often tracked alongside metabolic health (Essential).

Read together, these can help explain energy dips and weight changes — your clinician notes say what to discuss next.

All 3 panels

Hormones & libido

Total, free & bioavailable testosterone, SHBG, DHT, DHEA. Women’s adds estradiol, progesterone, FSH, LH

  • Total, free & bioavailable testosterone — How much is made, and how much your body can actually use.
  • SHBG — The protein that binds testosterone and affects how much is free.
  • DHT & DHEA — Related hormones read alongside testosterone.
  • Estradiol, progesterone, FSH & LH — Key cycle and menopause hormones (Women's).

Hormones are read as a set, not one number in isolation.

Men's & Women's

Thyroid & stress

TSH in every panel. Free T3, Free T4 and morning cortisol in Men’s & Women’s

  • TSH — The signal your brain sends your thyroid (every panel).
  • Free T3 & Free T4 — The active thyroid hormones (Men's & Women's).
  • Morning cortisol — A snapshot of your main stress hormone (Men's & Women's).

Together these can help explain tiredness, weight changes and feeling run-down.

TSH: all · full thyroid: M & W

Heart

Full lipid panel in every panel. hsCRP and homocysteine in Essential

  • Full lipid panel — Total, LDL, HDL and non-HDL cholesterol, triglycerides and ratios (every panel).
  • hsCRP — A marker of low-grade inflammation (Essential).
  • Homocysteine — An amino acid tracked for heart health (Essential).

Helps you track heart-health markers over time with your clinician.

All 3 · inflammation: Essential

Fatigue & nutrients

Vitamin D, B12, iron, ferritin, TIBC, iron saturation. Folate (RBC) in Essential & Women’s

  • Vitamin D & B12 — Common gaps that can go along with low energy.
  • Iron, ferritin, TIBC & saturation — Your iron stores and how well iron moves around your body.
  • Folate (RBC) — Included in Essential & Women's.

Spot common nutrient gaps that can sit behind fatigue.

All 3 panels

Everyday baseline

Kidney (9), liver (8) and a full blood count with differential (33)

  • Kidney (9) — Creatinine, eGFR, BUN and electrolytes.
  • Liver (8) — ALT, AST, ALP, albumin and bilirubin.
  • Full blood count (33) — Red cells, white cells and platelets.

The everyday baseline every panel includes.

Everything you'd ask a clinician.

Honest answers about labs, results, and what happens next. Have a question we missed? Message a clinician once you're inside.

Where do I get my blood drawn?
After checkout, your clinician-signed lab order appears in your portal. Walk into any of 2,000+ Quest Diagnostics locations near you — most don't need an appointment, or you can book a time online.
How fast do I get results?
Most panels come back from the lab in 3 to 5 business days. As soon as they're in, a clinician reviews every marker, writes your interpretation, and pushes your personalized plan to your portal. Typically within 1 to 2 business days of results landing.
Do I need insurance?
No. Our blood panels are direct-pay, with one transparent price that includes the lab draw, clinician interpretation, your personalized plan, and permanent access to your data. We don't bill insurance.
What if my labs show something concerning?
If a marker is significantly out of range. Or if a pattern suggests a condition that needs more workup. Your clinician will flag it directly in your interpretation, recommend next steps (additional testing, treatment, or in-person referral), and connect you with a physician on our platform if treatment is appropriate.
Can I retest later to track progress?
Yes. And we recommend it. Most members retest at 90 or 180 days to see how nutrition, supplements, and lifestyle changes have moved the needle. Your portal shows side-by-side comparisons of every biomarker over time, so you can see exactly what's improving.
Are these supplements pharmaceutical-grade?
Yes. We dispense through Fullscript. The same dispensary used by tens of thousands of clinical practices. Which gives you direct access to physician-grade brands at member pricing. Every recommendation is tied to a marker on your lab, not a guess.
