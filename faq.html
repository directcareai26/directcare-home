FAQ

What is DirectCare AI?

DirectCare AI is a telehealth platform providing personalized, AI-driven healthcare across men's and women's health, hormone therapy, sexual health, weight management, and hair restoration — all delivered remotely with US-licensed clinicians.

What services do you offer?

The platform includes hormone replacement therapy (HRT), testosterone replacement therapy (TRT), GLP-1 weight loss treatments, sexual health solutions, hair restoration, peptides, supplements, blood labs, and remote patient monitoring — covering both DTC care and provider/institution-side programs.

What types of hormone replacement therapy do you offer?

Personalized HRT for both men and women, addressing hormonal imbalances like low energy, mood swings, weight changes, and shifts in libido. Each plan is built off lab work and clinician review — not a one-size-fits-all protocol.

How does the consultation process work?

You complete a medical-history form online, then a US-licensed provider reviews your information and (where appropriate) consults with you to develop a customized treatment plan. If approved, your medications and any monitoring tools ship to your door.

How do I get started with DirectCare AI?

Start with a quick online medical-history form. Consult with a licensed provider, and receive a personalized treatment plan. Begin here — insurance is not required.

How are medications delivered?

Medications ship directly to your address in discreet packaging. Free shipping is included in all programs, and refills are handled automatically once your provider authorizes them.

How do I know if DirectCare AI is right for me?

Complete the quick medical-history form to determine eligibility. There's no commitment to start, and you'll know within a short turnaround whether the program is appropriate for your situation.

Is there a subscription or membership fee?

No. DirectCare AI operates on a pay-as-you-go model with no hidden fees. You only pay for the care you receive.

Do I need insurance to use DirectCare AI?

No — this is a cash-pay model with transparent, upfront pricing. For our RPM and CCM programs, however, Medicare Part B coverage typically applies.

Are your treatments safe and effective?

Yes. All treatments are developed and prescribed by licensed healthcare providers based on your individual evaluation. We use US-licensed compounding pharmacies and follow FDA guidance. See our Safety page for important warnings and side-effect information for GLP-1 medications and other prescriptions.

Who are the providers at DirectCare AI?

Licensed physicians and nurse practitioners specialized in the conditions we treat. Each clinician reviews your medical history and personally signs off on your treatment plan.

What is testosterone replacement therapy, and who is it for?

TRT is designed for men with symptoms of low testosterone — fatigue, low libido, decreased muscle mass, brain fog. Personalized plans help restore hormonal balance and vitality, with monitoring through quarterly bloodwork.
