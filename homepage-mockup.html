Everything tagged with a dashed border above is a placeholder. To take this mockup production-ready, here's the asset list — most can come from Firefly prompts, the rest need real photography or a designer.

  1. 2 hero twin-card product images Cropped close-ups: (a) HRT — gel pump or estradiol patch on cream linen, soft morning light · (b) TRT — testosterone vial + syringe on a lavender-tinted backdrop. Square 1:1. Firefly: product photography on cream/lavender backdrop, soft natural light, square aspect ratio.
  2. 4 medication product tiles Stylized product shots on solid pastel backgrounds, isolated: Tirzepatide pen, Semaglutide vial, Enclomiphene tablet, Surge Max bottle. 1:1 aspect ratio, each tile a different brand-friendly pastel.
  3. Patient-dashboard phone mockup iPhone screen mocking up the DirectCare AI portal — biomarker chart, clinician message thread, prescription tracker. Used in the "Get your labs" section. Can be designed in Figma + exported as a transparent PNG.
  4. 4 clinician headshots Real photography of your actual clinicians or vetted stock equivalents. 1:1 crop, neutral background, professional but warm. If you don't have real headshots yet, commission a single shoot day with all 4 clinicians.
  5. Bottom-CTA lifestyle photo Single human portrait, sunlit, warm color grade, looking off-camera with a soft smile. Used inside the dark-eggplant guide-download block. Firefly: editorial portrait of [age/identity] in warm afternoon light, soft confident expression, 4:5 portrait.
  6. 4 quick-link category icons OR thumbnails Currently rendered as line-SVG icons. If you'd rather have small photo thumbnails (Hims uses pill/syringe/dropper imagery), commission 40×40 icon photography for: Better sex · Regrow hair · Boost testosterone · Get a health check.
  7. Dark-hero promo product/scene visual Optional. The current dark promo uses a gradient background only. If you want to add product imagery (the Hims version shows GLP-1 pens floating in the dark), you'll need a transparent-background PNG of a pen + vial cluster styled for the dark space.
  8. Cream-block TRT featured visual (optional) A close-up of an enclomiphene tablet + lab tube + phone showing T levels — composed flat-lay on a cream linen background. Adds a real-product anchor to the otherwise text-only TRT featured block.
  9. Top-strip seasonal promo banner The yellow strip across the top is text-only right now. Could be lavender-gradient with a small icon. Decide later — easy CSS swap.
  10. Updated favicon set (optional) If you're shifting palette to warm cream + deep eggplant, refresh the favicon/apple-touch-icon to match the new ink color so it doesn't look out of place in browser tabs.
