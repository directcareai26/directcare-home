Is this for you?

HRT is not a wellness fad.
It's hormone medicine.

A US-licensed clinician decides if HRT is safe and right for you. Here's how to tell if you're a fit before you take the free evaluation.

You're a fit

This is for you if…

  • ✓ You're a woman in perimenopause, menopause or postmenopause
  • ✓ You're a postpartum or breastfeeding-finished mother with persistent low mood, brain fog, or low libido
  • ✓ Hot flashes, night sweats, sleep issues, or vaginal dryness are affecting your life
  • ✓ You want bioidentical hormones dosed to YOUR labs — not a one-size-fits-all dose
  • ✓ You're a man with low testosterone confirmed by labs (see TRT)
  • ✓ You want a clinician on call between visits
Not a fit

This is NOT for you if…

  • ✕ You're pregnant, recently postpartum (< 6 weeks), or actively breastfeeding
  • ✕ You have active or recent (within 5 years) hormone-sensitive cancer (breast, ovarian, endometrial)
  • ✕ You have a history of unprovoked blood clots, stroke, or active liver disease
  • ✕ You want a 30-day fix — hormone optimization takes 8–12 weeks to feel and several months to titrate
  • ✕ You're not willing to share medical history and complete a lab panel

Common questions

Everything you'd ask a hormone specialist.

Is bioidentical HRT safe?
For most healthy women within 10 years of menopause, the evidence (re-evaluated since the WHI study) supports HRT for relief of vasomotor symptoms, bone protection, and quality of life. The North American Menopause Society endorses HRT as first-line for symptomatic women under 60. Your clinician will personally rule out contraindications.
Bioidentical vs. synthetic hormones — what's the difference?
Bioidentical means the molecule has the same chemical structure as the hormones your body naturally produces (estradiol, progesterone, testosterone). Synthetic hormones (like medroxyprogesterone) have a different structure and a different side effect profile. We prescribe bioidentical exclusively.
How long until I feel different?
Hot flashes and sleep typically improve within 2–4 weeks. Mood, energy, libido and cognition take 8–12 weeks. Bone density and skin/hair benefits accrue over 6+ months. We re-check labs and adjust at 8–12 weeks to dial in your dose.
Do I need labs before starting?
Yes. Baseline labs (FSH, estradiol, total + free testosterone, SHBG, DHEA-S, TSH/free T3/free T4, lipids, fasting glucose, vitamin D) let your clinician dose to your actual physiology — not a guess. We coordinate the at-home or in-lab draw for you.
Is this covered by insurance?
No — DirectCare AI is direct-pay. That said, many patients pay less than what insurance copays cost on branded HRT, because compounded preparations skip the brand markup. Your prescription is also accepted by HSA/FSA accounts in most states.
What if I'm a man — can I get TRT here?
Yes. See our Men's TRT page — testosterone replacement for men is a separate protocol with its own intake, labs (total T, free T, SHBG, estradiol, hematocrit) and follow-up cadence.
