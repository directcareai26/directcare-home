Common questions

Everything you'd ask a clinician.

Honest answers, before you start. Have a question we missed? Message a clinician once you're inside.

Is DirectCare AI a real medical platform?
Yes. DirectCare AI is a HIPAA-compliant telehealth platform. Every prescription is reviewed and authorized by a US-licensed, board-certified clinician. We don't generate prescriptions with AI — we use technology to streamline intake, clinician workflow, and refills, but a human clinician makes every clinical decision.
Do I need insurance?
No. Our weight loss, HRT, TRT, sexual health, hair, and supplement programs are direct-pay — typically less than what insurance copays cost on the same medications. Our Chronic Condition Care program (RPM) is covered by Medicare and most major insurance.
What happens if I don't qualify?
You're not charged. The free evaluation is genuinely free. If a clinician decides a protocol isn't safe or appropriate for you, we'll tell you why — and we'll often recommend a better alternative (different protocol, lab workup first, or referral to in-person care).
Are the medications FDA-approved?
The active ingredients in every protocol are individually FDA-approved (e.g. Finasteride, Minoxidil, semaglutide, tirzepatide, estradiol, testosterone cypionate). Some formulations are compounded by a US-licensed pharmacy at clinician-specified strengths, which is a regulated practice but not "FDA-approved as a finished product." This is the same legal framework used by every major telehealth platform offering personalized compounded medication.
How fast does it ship?
After a clinician approves your protocol, your order ships in plain, unmarked packaging within a few business days. Refills auto-renew on your schedule — you can pause, adjust, or cancel anytime from your patient portal.
Can I message my clinician?
Yes — direct, async messaging with your assigned clinician is included in every program. Replies typically arrive the next business day. For TRT and HRT, we re-titrate based on labs at regular intervals once you're started.
