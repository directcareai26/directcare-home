I.Introduction

By using the telehealth services provided by Meta Digital Developers LLC DBA Direct Care DBA DirectCare AI (“DirectCare AI”) through its website, mobile apps, or other telehealth technologies (collectively, the “DirectCare AI Platform”), you are acknowledging and agreeing to this Telehealth Informed Consent. II.What Is Telehealth?

Telehealth delivers healthcare services using electronic communication technologies to connect healthcare professionals and patients in different locations. Telehealth may include:

  • Diagnosis, treatment, follow-up, and patient education.
  • Modalities such as real-time video and audio, asynchronous (store-and-forward) interactions, and other technologies.

This consent outlines the methods, benefits, risks, and limitations of using telehealth to address your healthcare needs.

III.Possible Benefits of Telehealth

  • Convenient access to healthcare services from the comfort of your home.
  • Reduced need for in-office visits, including follow-up care.
  • Flexible scheduling of consultations.

For follow-up care, contact us via the DirectCare AI Platform or call 888-298-6718, available Monday through Sunday from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST.

IV.Possible Risks of Telehealth

  • Insufficient information may prevent accurate diagnosis or treatment.
  • Technical failures (e.g., internet disruption) may delay care.
  • Telehealth may not be suitable for all conditions; Providers will determine appropriateness.
  • In rare cases, incomplete medical records or poor data transmission may lead to adverse outcomes, such as drug interactions or allergic reactions.

If technical issues arise, contact us at 888-298-6718 during the hours listed above.
