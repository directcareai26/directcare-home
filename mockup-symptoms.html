Mockup B — Colorful tile grid (static)

Replaces the existing symptom grid one-for-one but each card gets a unique pastel background color and a half-card cutout figure bleeding out the bottom-right. Big, scannable, no animation — every symptom is visible at a glance. Best for the existing symptom grid slot. Hover lifts the card 6px and the figure scales 1.06×. This is the closest match to how Hers handles their product cards on /weight-loss/.

Mockup C — Rotating words behind central figure

Three women centered in the band, 22 hormonal-life-stage words drifting in BOTH directions across the background at varied sizes / speeds / opacities (some bold purple, some soft eggplant, some deep), and animated white-light sparkles + diagonal shine sweep over the group — gives the whole banner a magical "you're seen, this is real, we have answers" feel. Mockup D — 3D pop-out (heads above the band)

Exactly Mockup C's rotating words + sparkles + shine + scroll-into-frame animation — BUT the colored gradient band is intentionally shorter than the figures, so the women's heads and upper shoulders stand above the band's top edge. Creates a 3D pop-out effect: as you scroll, the figures rise into frame and visibly extend out of the colored container. The rotating words and sparkles stay clipped inside the band only — they never appear above the heads.
