Mockup A — Hers-style hero carousel

One big card. Auto-rotates through all 8 symptoms every 5 seconds. Pastel background color shifts per symptom (lavender, peach, mint, coral, sky, gold, rose, sage). The cutout figure fades in/scales from 0.96, headline rotates up with the gradient italic. Best for the top of the page — replaces the static "Sound familiar?" headline with a living, breathing entry point. Mobile: stacks figure under text, same rotation.

Mockup B — Colorful tile grid (static)

Replaces the existing symptom grid one-for-one but each card gets a unique pastel background color and a half-card cutout figure bleeding out the bottom-right. Big, scannable, no animation — every symptom is visible at a glance. Best for the existing symptom grid slot. Hover lifts the card 6px and the figure scales 1.06×. This is the closest match to how Hers handles their product cards on /weight-loss/.

Mockup C — Rotating words behind central figure

One central figure stays in place. The 8 symptom words drift across the background in slow horizontal motion, each at a different vertical position, creating a kinetic-typography "she's living through all of these" effect. Below her, a floating glass pill shows the current symptom + a clinician-friendly framing. Best as a standalone hero piece or campaign landing. More distinctive than Mockups A or B — but only works if your figure has strong centered framing.
