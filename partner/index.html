Partner rules

These are conditions of the program, not suggestions. Partners who do not follow them are removed from the program and forfeit unpaid commissions.

You must disclose that you are paid.

Every time you promote DirectCare AI — in the same post, video, message or conversation, before the link — say clearly that you earn a commission on referrals. “#ad”, “paid partner”, or “I get paid if you sign up” all work. This is required by the FTC and it is a condition of the program.

You are not a clinician and must not act like one.

You may not give medical advice, interpret anyone’s labs or symptoms, recommend a specific medication or dose, tell anyone they qualify for a program, or suggest anyone stop or change a treatment.

Never promise a result.

Do not claim or imply any outcome — weight lost, hormone levels, hair regrown, or performance of any kind.

Never position DirectCare AI as a performance aid.

DirectCare AI programs are prescribed only for clinical indications. You may not present any program as a physique, competition or athletic-performance product, or imply it is an alternative to anything used for those purposes.

Do not target individuals by health condition.

Promote to your audience generally. Do not single out a specific person because you believe they have a medical condition.

Do not speak for DirectCare AI.

You are an independent partner, not an employee, agent or representative, and you cannot make commitments on our behalf.

Clinician independence

Referral commissions have no bearing on clinical decisions. Clinicians evaluate every patient independently and prescribe only what is clinically appropriate for that person. Partners are paid for marketing, not for any treatment decision, and a partner’s commission never changes what a clinician recommends. Not everyone who starts an evaluation qualifies for treatment.

If you want to try it yourself

Partner commissions are for referrals. If you want to start a program yourself, use the program pages like any other patient.
