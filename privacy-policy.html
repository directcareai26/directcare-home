II.Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

1. Personal Information

Information that identifies you, including:

  • Name, address, email, phone number
  • Date of birth, gender
  • Account credentials
  • Billing and payment information

2. Health Information

Information related to your health and care, including:

  • Medical history, conditions, and symptoms
  • Device data (e.g., glucose, blood pressure, weight)
  • Communications with care teams or AI systems

Health information may constitute Protected Health Information (PHI) and is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices.

3. Device and Usage Data

Automatically collected data:

  • IP address, browser type, device identifiers
  • Usage activity, session logs, timestamps
  • Referring URLs and navigation patterns

4. Communications Data

  • Messages via chat, SMS, email, or voice
  • Customer support interactions
  • AI interaction transcripts (where applicable)

5. Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

  • Healthcare providers and clinical partners (e.g., OpenLoop)
  • Device and data integrations (e.g., wearables)
  • Payment processors
  • Analytics providers

III.How We Collect Information

We collect information:

  • Directly from you (forms, onboarding, communications)
  • Automatically (cookies, analytics tools, logs)
  • From third parties (providers, integrations, partners)
