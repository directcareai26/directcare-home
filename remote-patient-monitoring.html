How it works

Five steps. One connected workflow.

From enrollment to claim — one platform handles the whole loop.

Enroll & provision

Identify and consent the patient. Distribute and connect their cellular device kit — no Bluetooth pairing required.

Plan & educate

Walk the patient through their device, set goals, build their engagement plan, and grant DirectCare app access.

Engage & measure

The patient takes daily measurements through their device and receives customized health content and reminders.

Monitor & intervene

Clinical team monitors readings and alerts, messages the patient directly, and escalates to the provider on protocol.

Report & bill

Care documentation flows back to the provider; claims auto-generate for billing inside the EHR.
