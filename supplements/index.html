Common questions

What you'd ask a clinical pharmacist.

Are these supplements FDA-approved?
Supplements are not FDA-approved as a finished product — that's true of every supplement on the market. What we DO have is third-party testing on every batch (identity, purity, heavy metals, microbial), manufacturing in an FDA-registered cGMP facility, and physician-led formulation based on published research.
Do I need a prescription?
No. Our supplements are dietary supplements and don't require a prescription. You can order them with or without an existing DirectCare AI protocol.
Can I take these alongside my prescription medications?
Most of them, yes — but some ingredients (berberine, nattokinase, vitamin K) interact with specific prescription drugs. The intake form asks about your current medications and our clinician flags any interaction concerns before your first order ships.
How fast will I feel something?
Varies by category. Sleep, focus and energy stacks typically deliver within 1–2 weeks. Foundation, hormone support and longevity stacks take 4–8 weeks. Cardio and gut stacks take 8–12 weeks (we recheck labs at that point).
Are these covered by HSA/FSA?
Some are eligible (when prescribed by a clinician for a specific medical purpose). Our patient portal can issue a Letter of Medical Necessity for HSA/FSA reimbursement on most stacks.
How does shipping work?
Plain, unmarked packaging in 2–4 business days. Subscriptions auto-renew on your schedule (default monthly). Pause, skip, or cancel anytime from your patient portal.
