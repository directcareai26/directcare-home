Common questions.

Straight answers from the same clinicians who write your protocol.

How does GLP-1 weight loss work here?

Compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide, dosed weekly, prescribed by a US-licensed clinician after a 5-minute intake. Every patient is paired with a Certified Medical Assistant Care Coach from day one for accountability and side-effect support. Plans start at $269/month for the compounded formula plus clinician access.

Am I a candidate for HRT?

Most women in perimenopause (typically late 30s through mid 50s) or post-menopause are candidates. We screen against contraindications like active breast cancer, untreated DVT/PE, or unexplained vaginal bleeding during your intake. A US-licensed clinician reviews every file before any prescription is written.

What's in your blood lab panel?

Our $196 comprehensive panel covers 80+ biomarkers across hormones (estradiol, progesterone, testosterone, FSH, LH, thyroid full panel), metabolic health (HbA1c, insulin, fasting glucose), lipids (full lipoprotein panel), inflammation (hs-CRP), iron, vitamins (D, B12, folate), and a CBC. Results in 3–5 days with a personalized roadmap from a US-licensed clinician.

What's in a typical HRT protocol?

Most protocols include estradiol (patch, gel, or oral), progesterone (oral micronized or topical, prescribed if you still have a uterus), and — when labs and symptoms support it — low-dose testosterone. Vaginal estrogen may be added for localized symptoms. Every dose is titrated to your bloodwork.

Does hair regrowth treatment actually work for women?

Yes, when matched to the underlying cause. Our female-pattern protocols combine topical and oral options — minoxidil, spironolactone (for androgen-driven loss), topical dutasteride, tretinoin, fluocinolone, and biotin support. Most women see new growth at 4–6 months. Severe shedding (telogen effluvium) usually resolves once the upstream hormonal trigger is corrected.

Are your supplements physician-formulated?

Yes — through our Fullscript partnership, your clinician builds a personalized supplement stack based on your blood panel results. Brands are practitioner-grade (Thorne, Pure Encapsulations, Designs for Health, etc.), not consumer drugstore quality. You see exactly which biomarker each supplement is targeting.

Is HRT safe?

For most healthy women under 60 (or within 10 years of menopause), modern bioidentical HRT has a favorable risk profile per the latest NAMS and ACOG guidance. We screen for personal and family risk factors during intake, and your clinician monitors you through ongoing labs.

Do you accept insurance?

We're cash-pay (no insurance hoops or prior auths), but we can provide an itemized receipt for FSA/HSA reimbursement. Most patients find total monthly cost lower than the copays + lab fees they'd see going through insurance.

Can I message my clinician between visits?

Yes — your DirectCare AI portal includes asynchronous messaging with your care team. Side-effect questions, dose-adjustment requests, and routine refills all happen through the portal, no extra fees.

What if a protocol isn't right for me?

If labs or history rule out one pathway, your clinician walks you through alternatives — non-hormonal symptom support, different GLP-1 dosing, supplement-first approaches, or lifestyle protocols. The intake is free; you don't pay until you start a protocol.
